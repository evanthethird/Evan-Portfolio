What it does

  • Press Button A to input Morse code (dot/dash)
  • Press Button B to send the code wirelessly
  • Receiver decodes the signal and displays the letter

What it demonstrates

  • Radio communication between devices
  • Morse code encoding and decoding
  • Input handling and state management
  • Real-time data transmission

def on_button_pressed_a():
    global Time
    if not (input.button_is_pressed(Button.A)):
        if Time < 0.3:
            radio.send_number(0)  # Dot
            basic.show_leds("""
                . . . . .
                . # # # .
                . # # # .
                . # # # .
                . . . . .
                """)
            basic.clear_screen()
        if Time >= 0.3:
            radio.send_number(1)  # Dash
            basic.show_leds("""
                . . . . .
                . . . . .
                . # # # .
                . . . . .
                . . . . .
                """)
            basic.clear_screen()
        Time = 0
